Dragon Poppers - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Introduction

Dragon Poppers ("we", "our", or "the app") is a puzzle game developed by Tofe Media. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Dragon Poppers does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage and are not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple Game Center - For leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Advertising Networks - The app may display ads through third-party ad networks. These networks may collect anonymous device information for ad targeting. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

In-App Purchases

The app offers in-app purchases processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Dragon Poppers does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require account creation or personal data entry.

Data Storage

All game data (level progress, settings, currency) is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
