Support

Help with gameplay, local progress, audio, haptics, accessibility settings, and optional Game Center features.

Email support

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the app is updated and restart it once.
  • Keep the device in landscape orientation.
  • For leaderboard or achievement issues, check that Game Center is signed in.
  • For sound or haptic issues, check both the in-game settings and device settings.
  • Core gameplay works offline; Game Center requires an internet connection.

Local progress

Progress is stored locally on the device. Removing the app may remove its local data. Include whether the issue followed an update, reinstall, or device change when contacting support.

What to include

Please send the app version, device model, iOS version, a short description of what happened, steps to reproduce it, and a screenshot when relevant. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
