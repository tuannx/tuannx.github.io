Sea Fish Shooter

Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026

Sea Fish Shooter is designed to work without a developer account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or custom game server.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or use personal data from Sea Fish Shooter. The app does not use third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, scores, unlocked content, language, audio, haptic, and accessibility preferences are stored locally on your device. We cannot access this local data. It remains until you reset the app data or remove the app, subject to device backups managed by you or Apple.

Apple Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you enable it, Apple may process your Game Center profile, achievements, and leaderboard activity under Apple's Privacy Policy. Cancelling or disabling Game Center does not block core gameplay.

Device access and payments

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health, or fitness information. Version 2.0.0 does not expose purchase or subscription controls. We do not receive payment-card information.

Children's privacy

The app does not require account creation or personal-data entry, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed behavior is released. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
